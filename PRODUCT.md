# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The FH Mumbai office team that arranges hotels for its ziyarat groups in Najaf and Karbala. They work mostly on a laptop or desktop at a desk, next to NKERP, the Excel plan and the messages they send to the hotels. A phone is the secondary screen.

## Product Purpose

Pull every approved FH Mumbai tour that arranges its own hotel from NKERP, then place each group in Ruya, Araf or Hotel C, night by night, so the extra hotels can be booked in advance. Success is knowing, before a group lands, which hotel and which rooms it has, and having the room-request message ready to send.

The four jobs that matter most, all of equal weight (confirmed by the owner):

1. See which nights overflow: when Ruya is full and which groups go to Araf or Hotel C.
2. Edit hotel pax and rooms: change hotel pax, type room requests, choose exact Ruya rooms.
3. Copy the room-request messages for the hotels.
4. Review what changed in NKERP since the last pull, and plan pending approvals.

## Positioning

The plan is built from live NKERP data and the hotel's real room list: a group gets exactly the Ruya rooms it asked for or moves whole to the next hotel, never split and never moved mid-stay.

## Operating Context

- NKERP is the source of tours, guest lists and sub-venue plans. Access is read-only.
- The Excel export mirrors the app: summary per hotel, daily plan, bookings to make, groups.
- Hotel messages follow the team's fixed text format and are copied out of the app.
- Edits are shared by everyone and saved as they are made. There is no login.
- A scheduled check pulls NKERP every 15 minutes, highlights tour changes and emails them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three tabs: Najaf (the full planner), Karbala Hotel Messages (messages only, no room plan), Najaf Approvals (proposes stays for pending tours).
- Terms the team uses: tour, group leader, hotel pax, Faiz accommodation, subvenue, pull, re-approval.
- Hotels fill in order: Ruya (room by room), Araf (bed count), Hotel C (no limit). The three hotels can be renamed.
- Room requests are typed bed size first (`2x6` is six 2-bed rooms).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: FH-MUM NJF Hotel Accommodation Planner.
- Plain, specific copy in the team's own words. No marketing language inside the tool.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real data comes only from NKERP. "Sample groups" are made up and labelled as such.
- No logo, photography or brand guidelines exist. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The plan is the product: occupancy and overflow are visible before anything else.
2. Every tour is recognisable at a glance wherever it appears.
3. Edits are immediate and shared, and the screen always says whether they are saved.
4. Warnings sit where the decision is made, never off-screen.
5. One way to do each thing, the same on every tab.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: text contrast of at least 4.5:1, full keyboard use including side panels, and reduced motion respected.
